# Course Feedback & Evaluation

## 1. Course Evaluation Questions
- Rate the clarity of explanations (1–5)
- Rate the depth of technical content (1–5)
- Rate usefulness of exercises & projects (1–5)
- Rate quality of code examples (1–5)

- Suggested enhancements to exercises or solutions
